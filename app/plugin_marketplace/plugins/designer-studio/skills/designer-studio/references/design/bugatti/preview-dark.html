Design System / Couture Automotive

Design System Inspiration of Bugatti

Cinematic negative space, monumental display typography, and a monochrome palette that refuses to compete with the product. Generated from DESIGN.md.

Section 01 / Palette

Color Palette

Velvet black canvas, showroom white type, one gray for the quiet moments. No accents. The product provides the color.

Primary
Velvet Black
#000000
The entire canvas. A display-stand surface — the way a jewelry brand treats black velvet.
Showroom White
#FFFFFF
All text, all borders, all CTAs. The only color at rest on the chrome.
Secondary
Silver Mist
#999999
The one gray. Tertiary text, disabled states, secondary button borders. Never a color — just quieter white.
Accent
None
—
Bugatti does not use accent colors. The car paint is the accent. The chrome stays silent.
